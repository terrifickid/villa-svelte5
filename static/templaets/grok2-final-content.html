Our Story

Established in 2011 by Barbadian entrepreneur Lily Dash, Villabound evolved from her Caribbean villa rentals service, Caribound. Stemming from Dash's premium Barbados villa rental experiences, Villabound now offers a globally curated selection of exclusive villas for discerning clients.

Each villa, meticulously chosen, showcases a country's best features, with top amenities, staff, art, and unique additions like gyms and personal spas.

What Makes the Difference

Luxury is expected. What sets us apart is a team that operates in sync year-round. Shared standards and consistent training ensure your villa runs properly from arrival to departure.
