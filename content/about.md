Villabound was established in 2011 by a dynamic Barbadian entrepreneur Lily Dash. The company grew out of her initial company Caribound; a specialized service focused on Caribbean villa rentals.

In a few short years Lily took her experiences of villa rentals in the premium market of Barbados and applied them to markets across the globe. Villabound was designed to cater to the needs of a high-end, discerning and discreet clientele. As a result our service involves the sourcing of unique and exclusive villas around the world, meeting the demands of our clients schedules. Every villa is carefully cultivated and represents the finest selection each country has to offer. Boasting details such as high-end amenities, inclusion of staff, priceless art pieces, fantastic furnishings as well as unimaginable features from gyms to personal spas.
